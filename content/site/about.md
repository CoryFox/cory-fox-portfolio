My current work includes building reports with Excel Power Query and OData, checking data against reporting requirements, and documenting how solutions work so they can be maintained and handed over.

Earlier eLearning work at Relish gave me hands-on experience with Articulate Storyline and SCORM. Teaching strengthened my ability to sequence information, support different needs, and explain technical tasks clearly. Both inform how I approach learning technology and client support today.

I am comfortable working close to product, design, and engineering. I understand how systems are built, how workflows break down, and how to shape solutions that are usable and practical to deliver.
